# Pricing that matches how this is sold

Three ways to sell the same product. Use the first one to win the first five clients, the second
to reach buyers who will not talk to a salesperson, and the third only after clients start asking
"can you just host it for us?".

Market anchors (2025–26 published rates): AI booking + reminders builds run **$1,500–$3,500 setup
with $497–$1,200/month** retainers; agencies quote **$400–$5,000 per single workflow** and
**$5,000–$25,000 for an agent system**; booking scripts sell on marketplaces at **$15–$199** regular
and **$999** extended; rebrandable white-label app licences run **$2,000–$25,000** perpetual or
**$500–$3,000/month** hosted.

## 1. Done-for-you (primary, highest price)

| Tier | Includes | Setup | Monthly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | One location, booking chat on their site, email confirmations, 24h/1h reminders, dashboard | £1,500 | £199 |
| **Growth** *(default)* | Starter + CRM, cancel/reschedule, live updates, installable app, Resend/Twilio, monthly tuning | £2,900 | £399 |
| **Connected** | Growth + integration with their existing PMS/calendar (bridge, reconciliation) | £3,900 | £499 |
| **Multi-site / white-label** | Several locations, custom domain, branded, priority support | £6,000–£12,000 | £900–£2,000 |

Setup includes installation, branding, service/hours setup, provider accounts, test bookings and a
short handover call. Monthly covers hosting, monitoring, message retries, small copy/hours changes
and one improvement per month.

## 2. Digital product (source licence)

| Licence | Price | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal / single business | £249 | Owner-operators who self-host |
| Commercial (one client business) | £499 | Freelancers delivering for a client |
| Extended / white-label | £1,499–£4,999 | Agencies, multi-site groups |

Include a 14-day setup-support window. Sell through your own site with a merchant-of-record
checkout (VAT handled for you) or a marketplace for discovery — not both exclusively, marketplaces
usually forbid it.

Add-ons: installation service £499; integration build £600–£3,500 depending on the system;
branding pass £299; training video £199.

## 3. Managed hosting (per client instance)

One deployment per client, their own database, their own keys:

| Plan | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosted instance | £79–£149/mo | Includes hosting, backups, updates, uptime monitoring |
| Hosted + integration | £149–£299/mo | Includes bridge/reconciliation monitoring |
| White-label hosting | £299–£799/mo | Their domain and branding, you stay invisible |

This is the honest version of "SaaS" without pretending to be a multi-tenant platform: each
customer gets isolation, export and deletion by design.

## What the buyer pays directly

- AI: roughly **£0.002–£0.01 per conversation** on Flash-class models; a busy 300-conversation
  month is a few pounds.
- SMS: about **£0.04 per message** in the UK (Twilio), 1h reminder per appointment.
- Hosting: **£5–£20/month** for one instance.
- Domain and Resend: free tier is usually enough at small volume.

## ROI line that closes the deal

80 appointments a month at a 15% no-show rate is 12 empty slots. At £60 average value that is
**£720/month lost**. Recovering half of it pays a Growth retainer four times over. Ask for their
numbers and do the sum on the call — do not quote a generic percentage.

## Rules that protect the price

- Never discount the monthly to win the deal; shorten the **included scope** or the pilot instead.
- Pilot: 30 days at 50% of monthly, no setup discount, converts automatically to full price.
- No free integrations: integration work is where the unbilled hours hide.
- Quote in writing, list exclusions explicitly (content writing, ad campaigns, SEO, medical advice).
- Raise prices after every five clients; grandfather existing ones for 12 months.
